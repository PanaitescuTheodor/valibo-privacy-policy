# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data **Valibo** collects, how it's used, and what rights you have. This app is currently in closed beta testing.

**Data controller:** Theodor Panaitescu, operating as an individual (no registered business entity at this stage).
**Contact:** theodor.panaitescu@gmail.com

*This policy is written to be accurate and honest about current practices during beta testing. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before any public or commercial launch, this document should be reviewed by a qualified professional to ensure full legal compliance.*

---

## 1. What data we collect

- **Account information:** your email address and password (handled securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password), and an optional display name you can set yourself.
- **Item data you add:** product names, brands, categories, expiry information (dates or period-after-opening details), and status (e.g. opened, discarded) for items you choose to track.
- **Photos:** if you choose to add a photo of a product, it's stored securely and linked only to your account.
- **App preferences:** your selected theme (light/dark) and language (Romanian/English), stored on your device and/or your account.
- **Notification scheduling data:** to send you expiry reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your own device. This data does not leave your device or get sent to us as a "push" service — reminders are calculated and triggered by the app itself.

We do **not** currently collect: analytics data, advertising identifiers, location data, or any data through third-party trackers or ad networks. The app currently contains no ads and no analytics SDKs.

## 2. Why we collect it

We use your data solely to provide the app's core functionality:
- Authenticating you and keeping your data private to your account
- Storing and displaying the items you choose to track
- Sending you expiry reminders
- Remembering your preferences (theme, language)

## 3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using **Supabase**, a third-party database and infrastructure provider, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt region). Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf. You can review Supabase's own privacy and security practices at supabase.com.

Photos are stored in a private storage bucket, accessible only to your authenticated account — never publicly accessible.

## 4. How long we keep your data

- Your account and item data are kept for as long as your account is active.
- If you discard an item in the app, we keep a record of it (including its category and timing) rather than deleting it immediately — this may be used in the future to show you helpful statistics (e.g. how many products you've used up or wasted). Associated photos of discarded items may be removed after a retention period to manage storage, while the item record itself is kept.
- If you'd like your data deleted sooner, contact us (see below) and we'll handle this manually during the beta period — full self-service account deletion is planned but not yet built into the app.

## 5. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable Romanian data protection law, you have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Request deletion** of your data
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Object** to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights during the beta, contact us at the email above — we'll handle requests manually and promptly given the small scale of testing at this stage.

## 6. Security

We rely on Supabase's infrastructure security, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database-level access controls (Row Level Security) that ensure your data is only ever accessible to your own authenticated account.

## 7. Children's privacy

This app is not intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age required by applicable law in their country of residence.

## 8. Changes to this policy

As the app develops beyond beta — including if it expands to new categories, adds new features, or moves toward public/commercial release — this policy will be updated, and beta testers will be notified of material changes.

## 9. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Romania and applicable EU data protection regulation (GDPR).
